Landscape installation

Sweetwater Landscapes has the experience and ability to manage any size or scope of project. From commercial buildings to parks, we apply the same attention to detail to complete every project on schedule and on budget.

Our management team emphasizes coordination and teamwork with other on-site contractors — ensuring project deadlines are met safely, and that trades don't get in each other's way.

Landscape & lawn maintenance

Our maintenance division is dedicated to quality landscape maintenance for large or small commercial properties. Every program is customized to a property's specific needs, expectations, and budget.
