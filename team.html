RB

Rich Bessette

Owner & president

Richard graduated from East Carolina University in 1994 with a degree in Industrial Technology and Sales, and was active in the PKA fraternity. He started Triangle Maintenance Group in 2001 as sole owner and operator, then formed Sweetwater Landscapes in 2003. He enjoys growing and managing both the Maintenance and Turf Care divisions, and has an active family with his wife Kristy and their five boys.

  • 16+ years in the green industry
  • ECU, Industrial Technology & Sales
