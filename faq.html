Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, no runaround.

Pricing, contracts, licenses, timelines — the things people actually want to know before they call.

What areas do you serve?

Two regions of North Carolina: the Triangle (Raleigh, Apex, Cary, Holly Springs, and surrounding towns) from our Apex office, and greater Wilmington (Castle Hayne, New Hanover County, Leland) from our Castle Hayne office.

Do you take residential properties?

No — our lawn maintenance, turf management, and grounds care programs are exclusively for HOA communities, commercial properties, builders, and multi-unit developments. For fence installation, contact Kevin Papushak directly at (216) 401-5022.

How is landscape maintenance priced?

By maintained area, scope, and visit frequency. Our plans start around $495/month for a typical small commercial site. Use the instant estimate calculator on the plans page for a ballpark, then we confirm with a free on-site walk.

Do you require long-term contracts?

Maintenance agreements are typically annual so we can plan seasonal programs (turf applications, mulch, color rotations) properly. Installation and fencing projects are quoted per project with no ongoing commitment.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Sweetwater holds an NC Registered Landscape Contractor license, NC Licensed Irrigation Contractor license, NC Pesticide Applicator's license, and Storm Water BMP Inspection & Maintenance certification, and carries full liability coverage.

How fast can you install a fence?

After a consultation you'll usually have a quote within two business days, and most residential and light-commercial installs happen within two to four weeks depending on material availability and permitting.

Do you handle storm and hurricane cleanup?

Yes. Both offices run storm response crews during hurricane season, and maintenance contract clients get priority routing — Estate plan clients are first out.

Can you manage our irrigation system?

Yes — irrigation audits, repairs, spring startup, and winterization are all performed in-house under our NC irrigation contractor license. Signature and Estate plans include scheduled irrigation checks.

How do I get a quote?

Three ways: run the instant estimate calculator for a planning ballpark, send the contact form and we'll respond within one business day, or call 919-387-7103 (Triangle) / 910-623-1262 (Wilmington) to schedule a free site walk.

Are you hiring?

Almost always — crews grow every spring. See current openings for crew members, crew leaders, irrigation techs, fence installers, and account managers on our careers page.

Question we didn't cover?

One call routes you to a person, not a phone tree.
